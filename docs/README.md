# OctoAcme Project Management

## Purpose

This directory is the entry point for OctoAcme's project management process. The
guides apply to cross-functional work that delivers product features, services,
or integrations, with an emphasis on customer value, iterative delivery, clear
ownership, and data-informed decisions.

## Workflow Summary

1. **Initiate:** Validate the problem and desired outcome, identify stakeholders,
   define success metrics and an initial timeline, capture early risks, and
   obtain a go/no-go decision.
2. **Plan:** Kick off the approved initiative, prioritize and estimate a
   backlog, define acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, map
   dependencies and milestones, and establish a release plan and QA approach.
3. **Execute and track:** Deliver small increments through the project board,
   using reviews, CI tests and linting, approvals, and regular demos to maintain
   quality and visibility.
4. **Manage risks and communicate:** Keep the risk register current, review
   risks and dependencies in weekly syncs, share regular stakeholder updates,
   and escalate blockers through the PM, Product Lead, and sponsor as needed.
5. **Release and deploy:** Confirm acceptance criteria, CI and security checks,
   release notes, rollback planning, and smoke tests before staging and
   production deployment; verify and announce the release afterward.
6. **Improve continuously:** Hold retrospectives after sprints, releases,
   milestones, and incidents; assign and track a small set of action items in
   the backlog or issues.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communication.
Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and measure success.
Developers build and test the work, while QA validates quality and acceptance
criteria; stakeholders provide input and approvals.
